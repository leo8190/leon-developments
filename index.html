Products

Each one solves a single job end to end. The colored edge shows where it stands: yellow is live, blue is in beta, gray is a prototype.

  • TrasteFiel

    Live beta. Web app, free plan.

    Turns a guitar recording into an editable tablature draft. It separates the guitar from the mix, detects the notes and exports MusicXML and MIDI for MuseScore or Guitar Pro. If the detection is not reliable enough, it says so instead of handing over a wrong tab. An Android beta is with testers.

    Try TrasteFiel

  • RecordPress

    Live. Free tool.

    Turns a SmartSuite CSV export into a branded, client-ready PDF. Everything runs in the browser, so the data never leaves the user's device, and there is no sign-up.

    Open RecordPress

  • SpecPack PDF

    Beta. Setup slots open.

    Builds supplier-ready apparel tech packs, with measurements, bill of materials and colorways, from product data that teams already keep in Airtable, Glide or Google Sheets.

    See SpecPack PDF

  • Pausa Mía

    Prototype. In user testing.

    Short, personalized guided meditations that play in the browser, in Spanish, with a guest mode that needs no account.

    Try Pausa Mía
